# Sheboyganite well

Forever photo and video library for SheVegas. This repo is the source of truth. Not Google Photos. Not Apple Photos.

Live published stills: `live/images/`
Live published clips: `live/video/`
Incoming originals: `well/incoming/`

Index first. Do not dump the lifetime original archive into git. Batches from campus computers and exports land in `well/incoming/`. Catalog here. Originals stay in the well, not in a photo app.

Known incoming piles (not here yet):
- Google Takeout / Apple Photos export
- Drive archives off campus computers (Sheboygan civic, bars, parades, Packers, Midwest cities)

Do not invent captions. Filename and date only until Michael writes a line.
