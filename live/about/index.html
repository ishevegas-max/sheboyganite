sheboyganite.com

The funnel for people from here.

Mike Brunette launched SheVegas.com around 2005 from South 26th Street after an MFA in journalism at the University of Iowa. Sheboyganite is the front door: everything Sheboygan, for people from Sheboygan. SheVegas is the nickname that stuck.

Three desks sit behind this masthead — archive, civic, and night. Tell the desk what to add.

Method

Original source documents and verified first-hand accounts come before anyone's spin, including ours. Fun with a purpose.

Tip line

Mail ishevegas@gmail.com with a document, a correction, or a room that should be on the night desk.
