## 1. The story spine

A small city where the same two people who hired the city administrator later stripped his power and fired him, then settled for $230K — and the federal case against them survived a motion to dismiss.

Around that core, three supporting threads:

- **Money** flows through five tax-increment districts and a $1.14M lead-pipe bond.
- **Land** deals carry indemnification clauses that pit the city against its own neighboring town.
- **Press** that should have covered all of it shrank to mail-only delivery the same year the settlement hit.

This is a governance failure with a paper trail, not a scandal report.
