## 1. THE USER'S THESIS

> "The noise is purposeful. I am the laser that cuts through it."

Sheboygan's local "news" is not journalism — it is chamber content and City Hall content distributed through captured outlets (Sheboygan Sun, MySheboygan, formerly the Sheboygan Press). The actual governance happens in committee packets, bond documents, closed sessions, and resignation letters that never make the front page.

Not news aggregation. Forensic reading of government documents to expose the gap between what gets voted on and what gets reported.

> "Sheboygan is not having a slow news summer. Sheboygan is having a very busy summer that the press has agreed not to cover."

The laser is the document, not the headline.

## 4. THE MECHANISM

1. **The 2–3 week dead cycle.** August: councils go light, press goes lighter, serious money moves in committee. Flock dropped into the vacuum. Needs a three-year August control comparison (was Dim08 — never launched).
2. **The press is not a press.** Sheboygan Sun and MySheboygan are chamber funnels. Sheboygan Press did not vanish — it **shrank**: mail-only delivery from April 2024, staff cuts. Same year as the Wolf settlement. Atrophy, not disappearance.
3. **Board packing.** Salgado, Donahue, Brunette, Born, Thaichroen — hypothesis. Source each appointment before publishing the list as fact.
4. **Data-center referendum as pressure valve.** Appearance of public input. Failed (100+ short, extension denied). No post-mortem. Where did the Titi signatures go?
5. **DPW: equipment-rich, infrastructure-poor.** Loaders, forklift, aeration, UV for Motor Vehicle Division. Streets and sewers live in the resolutions, not the headlines. "Name That Truck" is the smile on the face of decay.
